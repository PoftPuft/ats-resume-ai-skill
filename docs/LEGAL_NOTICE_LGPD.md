# ⚖️ Privacy and Data Protection (LGPD/GDPR)

Using Artificial Intelligence for resume analysis and reformulation involves personal data processing. By using this skill, be aware of the following security and privacy guidelines:

## 1. Data Minimization

**DO NOT provide unnecessary sensitive data for professional screening.**
Avoid including in your resume or in the AI questionnaire responses information such as:

- SSN, ID, Driver's License, Passport, or Voter Registration numbers.
- Complete residential address (City and State are sufficient).
- Marital status, religion, sexual orientation, or date of birth.
- Personal photos (ATS systems don't process images and this can generate bias in recruitment).

## 2. Model Training

Public AI platforms (like free versions of ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude) may use data entered in conversations to train their foundational models.

- **If you want total privacy:** Access your chosen AI platform's settings and **disable conversation history** or **data usage for model training** before attaching your resume or answering the questionnaire.

## 3. Disclaimer

This skill is an educational, open-source system prompt. The creator of this repository has no access to, does not collect, and does not store any information sent by users to AI platforms. Responsibility for processing the data sent lies with the LLM provider company and the user themselves.
